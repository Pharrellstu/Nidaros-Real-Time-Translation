TODO – Live Auto-Captions POC for Wowza + Faster-Whisper (Wyoming)

Goal (POC)
- Use the linuxserver/faster-whisper container (Wyoming protocol on port 10300) to transcribe Dutch speech from our Wowza live stream and expose captions as an HLS WebVTT sidecar that players can select.

Simplified approach
1) Audio in: use ffmpeg to pull the Wowza HLS stream and output 16 kHz mono s16le PCM.
2) STT: send PCM to Faster-Whisper over the Wyoming protocol and receive text segments.
3) Captions: write rolling WebVTT segments (2–4s) and a small subtitle playlist (.m3u8). Serve via our API.
4) Player wiring: provide a composed master playlist endpoint that mirrors Wowza’s HLS master and adds a subtitles group pointing to our subtitle playlist.

Notes
- Keep it global/simple: one pipeline for the “main” stream (no per-stream config for now).
- Language: Dutch (WHISPER_LANG=nl). Model: start with small-int8 for speed.
- No Wowza plugin required; we don’t modify the Wowza container.
